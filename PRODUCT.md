# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployable as-is on GitHub Pages (repo SudoSerere/AntoineDIOKH). The specimen redesign replaced the previous site on main.

## Users

Recruiters and design leads hiring a Product Designer (CDI). They scan fast on desktop between other candidates, decide in seconds whether the work is worth opening, then open one case study (DigDash) and the CV.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Antoine Diokh, Product Designer at DigDash (Fuveau). Success: a design lead understands his role and level within one viewport, opens the DigDash case study or the CV, and writes to him.

## Positioning

A product designer who ships inside a BI platform (DigDash Enterprise visual theme, client dashboards) and also does print and 2D motion: research on one side, pixel care on the other. "Idées non conventionnelles, exécution minimaliste."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual FR (default) / EN with a toggle; every string has both versions.
- Light / dark theme toggle, defaulting to system preference, stored in localStorage (`ad_theme`).
- Interactive Figma-style "Fonts" panel that re-sets the "Product Designer" title in the chosen face: must survive.
- Eight projects open a detail view with external links (Figma prototypes, Drive/YouTube videos, live site, `digdash.html` case study).
- CV at `assets/CV_Antoine_DIOKH.pdf`.
- The narrator / "micro coupé" humour is not a binding commitment; factual copy stays.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads: a recruiter should reach a project in one scroll.
2. Show craft through the interface itself, not claims about it.
3. Everything bilingual and theme-aware, no exceptions.
4. Static, light, fast: no framework, no tracking.
